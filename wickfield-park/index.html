The Park

A country stay, minutes from town

Wickfield Park sits right at the edge of the Stillwater city limits on N Jardot Rd — far enough out that the evenings are genuinely quiet, close enough that campus, groceries and dinner are a short drive away.

We kept it small on purpose. Eleven sites means there's always room between rigs, the awning side stays open, and you're not listening to a neighbor's generator through the wall. It's a park built for people who want space — travelers passing through, contractors and travel nurses on a monthly stay, families in town for an Oklahoma State weekend, and snowbirds who'd rather not park in a lot.

Every site is a pull-through and every site is oversized, so a 45-foot coach with a toad pulls straight in, levels up, and stays hitched if you want it to.

And the power's on us. Electric is included in every rate — nightly, weekly or monthly — so you can run both air conditioners through an Oklahoma August without watching a meter.

Questions

Good to know

The things guests ask us most. Anything else, just call or send a note.

Is electricity included in the rate?

Yes. Every site has 50 amp service and electric is included — nightly, weekly or monthly. There's no metered power and no add-on fee, so a long summer stay doesn't come with a surprise bill.

Are all the sites really pull-through?

All eleven. You drive in and drive out, with room for a full-length coach plus a towed vehicle. If you're running something unusually long, call us and we'll confirm the fit before you book.

How far are you from OSU and Boone Pickens Stadium?

We're at 1919 N Jardot Rd on the edge of the Stillwater city limits — a short drive from campus and the stadium, but far enough out that game-weekend traffic isn't parked outside your door.

Why do nightly and weekly rates change?

Stillwater fills up around the Oklahoma State calendar — football Saturdays, Homecoming, graduation and move-in weekends. Nightly and weekly rates reflect the date you're booking. Put your dates into the booking calendar and you'll see the exact price before you reserve.

Are dogs allowed?

Absolutely — and they get their own fenced dog park on the property. We just ask that pets stay leashed elsewhere in the park and that you clean up after them.

Do you take monthly guests?

Yes, and monthly is our best value. It's popular with travel nurses, contractors, students and snowbirds. With only eleven sites, monthly availability is worth asking about early.

What's the WiFi like?

Free and park-wide, included with every stay. No daily code, no per-device charge.
