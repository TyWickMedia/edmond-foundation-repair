The Park

A country stay, minutes from town

Wickfield Park sits right at the edge of the Stillwater city limits on N Jardot Rd — far enough out that evenings are genuinely quiet, close enough that campus, groceries and dinner are a short drive away.

We kept it small on purpose. Eleven sites means there's always room between rigs, the awning side stays open, and you're never listening to a neighbor's generator through the wall. It's a park built for people who want space — long-haul travelers passing through, contractors on a monthly stay, families in town for an Oklahoma State weekend, and snowbirds who'd rather not park in a lot.

Every site is a pull-through and every site is oversized, so a 45-foot coach with a toad pulls straight in, levels up, and stays hitched if you want it to.
